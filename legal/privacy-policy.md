# Privacy Policy — PXLPOD Photobooth

_Last updated: August 17, 2026_

> This is the source text for the PxlPod app privacy policy. It mirrors the live
> page served at `/privacy` by the web app. Update both together, and revise the
> date and the operator contact before launch.

## The short version

The PxlPod app makes photo strips on your phone. The photos and strips you shoot
stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded to make a strip. A photo only leaves
your phone when you choose to share it, create a share link, or send it to print
— and only after you tap that action.

## Who we are

PXLPOD Photobooth operates the pop-up photobooth and this app. We are responsible
for the personal data described here. Questions? Email **hello@pxlpod.example**.

## What we collect, and when

- **Photos and strips** — created and stored on your device. We never receive
  them unless you use a sharing or printing action below.
- **Share links** — if you tap "Create a share link," the finished strip is
  uploaded so anyone with the link can view it. Share links expire automatically
  (about 30 days).
- **Print at a pop-up** — if you tap "Print at this pop-up," the finished strip is
  uploaded so our booth staff can print it. It is tied to that event and removed
  after the event window.
- **Booking inquiries** — if you use "Book us," we collect the details you enter
  (name, email and/or phone, and event details) so we can reply with a quote.
- **Approximate location** — only if you allow it, and only to check whether a
  pop-up is running near you. It is used on-device and not stored by us.

## What we do NOT do

- No guest accounts. You use the app without signing up.
- No microphone access. No advertising trackers. We don't sell your data.
- We don't upload your photos in the background or for analytics.

## How sharing works

"Save to device" writes a copy to your phone's photo library. "Share" hands the
image to whichever app you pick (Instagram, Messenger, etc.), which then handles
it under its own policy. Uploaded strips (share links and prints) are stored with
our infrastructure provider, Supabase, on our behalf.

## How long we keep things

- On-device photos and strips: until you delete them (More → Delete all my strips).
- Share links: expire automatically, roughly 30 days after creation.
- Print uploads: kept for the event, then cleared.
- Booking inquiries: kept while we handle your booking and our records require.

## Your choices

- Delete every strip the app holds at any time from the More screen.
- Revoke camera, photos, or location permission in your phone's Settings.
- Ask us to access or delete a booking inquiry, or a strip you shared or sent to
  print, by emailing **hello@pxlpod.example**.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Booking inquiries should be
submitted by an adult.

## Changes

We'll update this page if our handling changes and revise the date above.
Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
